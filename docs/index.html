What is the Gatineau Summer Festival?

The Gatineau Summer Festival provides all kinds of different attractions for people of all ages. Hosted at the wonderful Leamy Lake between the 11th to the 18th of July every year. Since 1974, the summer festival offers a fantastic experience for all those who wish to partake in the festivites. Our aim is to insure that the experiences you have at the Gatineau Festival will be one to remember for many years to come.

Fun for everyone

The Gatineau Sumer Festival is made to be enjoyed by all. Whether you want to go as group, with the family, with a friend or two, or even by yourself no matter what you will have the time of your life. You may even meet new people and experience fun beyond your wildest imagination as you laugh along with the friend you made along the way. The festival hosts many food stalls for all those that want to partake in the Gatineau delicacies.

Work as a team

Put your teamwork to the test with our exciting group challenges and aquatic games! The Gatineau Summer Festival brings people together to build lasting memories, cheer each other on, and accomplish great things as a team.
