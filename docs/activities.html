The grand challenge

Get ready for our signature waterfront showcase where teams build and race handmade vessel creations right along the shore! Whether you are competing for the top prize or cheering from the beach with family and friends, the grand challenge brings a smile to all.

So many options to choose from

Step right up and test your skills with a wide variety of classic carnival games and interactive outdoor stations designed for all ages! From high-striker strength tests to beanbag tosses and ring tosses, there are endless prizes to win and smiles to share as you explore everything our festival grounds have to offer.

Try so many different amazing food

Treat your taste buds to an incredible lineup of local food vendors and classic festival treats! From savory regional specialties to sweet summer snacks and refreshing beverages, our fantastic food stalls ensure you will never run out of delicious options to refuel between all your favorite events.
