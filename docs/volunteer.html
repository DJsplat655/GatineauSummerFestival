Want to gain experience working as a team?

Joining the Gatineau Summer Festival volunteer team is an incredible way to build hands-on experience, connect with amazing people, and give back to your local community! Volunteers assist with event setup, guest guidance, activity coordination, and supporting food vendor areas. You will gain valuable leadership skills while helping us deliver the ultimate summer experience for all of our guests.
