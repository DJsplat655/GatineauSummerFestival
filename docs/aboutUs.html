How the Gatineau Summer Festival was founded

Founded in 1974 by a passionate group of local organizers, the Gatineau Summer Festival was born out of a friendly competition with the famous Quebec Winter Carnival to prove that summer in Quebec isn't soething to ignore. What started as a modest weekend gathering at Leamy Lake Park has grown into a premiere summer destination celebrating regional culture, community spirit, and warm-weather outdoor fun. Our mission remains simple: to showcase the very best of Gatineau and deliver an unforgettable experience for visitors of all ages.
